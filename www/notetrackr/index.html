How Syncing Works:

  • Always Offline-First: Your notes save directly to this device instantly. You never have to wait for a loading spinner to finish writing.
  • Invisible Backup: When you have internet, notes quietly copy to your private cloud in the background.
  • Safe Device Merging: If you log in on a new device, it downloads your cloud notes and mixes them into your local history list. It never deletes a local note.
