### 📱 Android Home Screen Widget

Lifeblogging Hub includes a native Android widget to keep your latest mood right on your home screen.

**Installation Instructions:**
1. **Install the App:** Open Lifeblogging Hub in Chrome on your Android device. Tap the browser menu (three dots) and select **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
2. **Add the Widget:** Long-press any empty space on your Android home screen and select **"Widgets"**.
3. **Place It:** Scroll down to **Lifeblogging Hub**, tap it, and drag the "Latest Mood" widget onto your screen.

**How it Updates:**
* **Automatic Updates:** Android manages background updates automatically to preserve battery life (typically refreshing every 30-60 minutes). You do not need to keep the app open.
* **Manual Refresh:** To fetch your latest data instantly, tap the small refresh icon in the top right corner of the widget.
* **App Versions:** Whenever the core app receives a major native update, Android will silently update the widget container over Wi-Fi in the background.
